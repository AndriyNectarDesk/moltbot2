MIKEQUIET WATER

Three minutes on the lake before anyone else is up. Read the surface, wait for the float to go under, then hold your nerve.

HOLD wind up a cast
LET GO send it
TAP set the hook
HOLD reel it in
Read the water. Rings mean a fish just rose. Lily pads and the shade under the dock always hold something. A shimmer crossing the lake is the best water there is.
Learn the tells. A flurry of little taps is a sunfish. One solid thump is a bass. If the float just leans over and keeps going, something big has it — strike once it goes under, not before.
Keep it tight, but watch it. A tighter line brings the fish in faster — and reeling is also what tightens it. Before every run the fish gathers itself and the bar flashes LET GO. Let go then and you ride the run out. Hold on through it and the line snaps.
