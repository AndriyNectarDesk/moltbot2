How the prize works

Every game has its own board that resets each week — Monday morning to Sunday night. Win your game's board and you win that game's prize.

The joint board is different. In each game, first place is worth 10 points, second 6, third 3, and just posting a run is worth 1. Add up your points from all three games and the highest total wins the big prize — so playing your brother's or sister's game is worth doing.

If only one person plays a game all week, that game only gives out participation points. No free wins.

Scores come from your own computer, so Dad checks the board before paying anyone. He can see every time you beat your own score, when you did it, whether a score jumped further than it should have, and anything that looks too big to be real.
